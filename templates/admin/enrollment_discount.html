How Discount Works

  • • Discount is applied when a student selects an even number of courses (2, 4, 6, etc.)
  • • The discount amount is a fixed value in pounds (£)
  • • The discount is deducted from the subtotal before payment
  • • Example: If discount is £10 and student selects 2 courses for £200 total, they pay £190
